Introduction

Vaultrafy ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Vaultrafy application ("Application").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information Collection and Use

Local Storage of Information

Vaultrafy is designed with your privacy in mind. All information you store in Vaultrafy, including passwords, API keys, subscription details, and notes, is:

  • Stored locally on your device
  • Encrypted using industry-standard encryption methods
  • Never transmitted to our servers or any third parties
  • Only accessible with your master password

Information We Do Not Collect

Vaultrafy does not:

  • Collect personal information about you
  • Track your usage or behavior within the application
  • Use cookies or similar tracking technologies
  • Send your stored credentials to any external servers
  • Share your data with third parties

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • All sensitive information is encrypted using AES-256 encryption
  • Your master password is never stored; it is only used to derive the encryption key
  • Data is only decrypted temporarily in memory when you access it

Children's Privacy

Our Application is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
